# Patient Preparation Instructions by Visit Type

These are given to the patient at booking and repeated in the reminder.

## New patient intake

Arrive 15 minutes early. Bring a photo ID, insurance card, a list of current
medications with doses, and records from any prior practice if available. Plan
for 40 minutes.

## Annual physical

Fast for 8 hours beforehand if bloodwork is expected — the booking staff will
confirm with the provider's office. Bring a list of current medications. Wear
clothing that allows a blood pressure cuff on the upper arm.

## Post-operative check

Bring the discharge paperwork from the procedure. Do not remove dressings before
the visit. Arrange a ride home if the visit may involve sedation; routine post-op
checks do not.

## Telehealth follow-up

Test the video link the day before. Be somewhere private with a stable
connection. Have a current medication list and any home readings — blood
pressure, glucose, weight — available. Telehealth is not appropriate for visits
requiring a physical exam; if the provider determines an exam is needed, the
visit is converted to in-person and no charge applies to the telehealth portion.

## Procedure consultation

Bring imaging or lab results from outside facilities if they have not already
been sent. Bring a list of questions; consultations run 30 minutes and it is
common to run out of time.
